2026-11-13: **PCMCIA** built (`machine-amiga/src/pcmcia.rs`, 2 tests; Gayle decodes $600000–$9FFFFF and $A00000–$A5FFFF on the A1200, card status bits, card detect request → INT6; media slot 6 `SLOT_PCMCIA`, "PCMCIA", kind cartridge, so `--cart` reaches it). An SRAM card is an image of its common memory, kept in memory (it is RAM, so savestates hold it) and written back on flush/eject; its CIS is generated (CISTPL_DEVICE SRAM; with "DOS" at the image's start also CISTPL_FORMAT and CISTPL_GEOMETRY) unless the image starts with its own. Kickstart 40.068 takes a blank 1 MB card as fast RAM ($600220–$700000, `amiga_boot::a_pcmcia_sram_card_becomes_fast_ram`, with a savestate round trip) and boots Workbench 3.1 from a card holding the ADF as CC0: in 400 frames (`workbench_31_boots_from_a_pcmcia_card`). Not checked: inserting or removing a card while running (the detect request is raised, its handling not tried), card images from WinUAE or real cards, PrepCard-made CIS in common memory, I/O cards, the slot's access speed.

The user wrote the disks with the Greaseweazle through this session (`gw write --drive 0`, each read back byte-identical; the overwritten disks' ADFs kept in the session scratchpad) and photographed `scroll`, `scan` and `bypass` on the real A1200 the same day: the real machine agrees with WinUAE on the scroll limit and on BSCAN2/SSCAN2 and shows BYPASS as the raw value. Built from that in `chips-amiga` (2 new tests): the scroll limit of 64 pixels of the resolution (the AXIS band is gone), BSCAN2, SSCAN2 (compare without SH10, DMA on alternate lines), BYPASS (raw value as grey, the border black). mimic's pictures of all three probes now match the photos (scan line for line with WinUAE). `agatest.adf` boots on the A1200 (the disk shows as uninitialized: it has only a boot block, like the probes) but the screen stays black; WinUAE and mimic draw it. `tools/amiga_agaprobe.py ddf` is the same picture on a green COLOR00 to tell whether the Copper runs at all. Later the same day: the black screen was the A1200's analogue RGB output (first a broken blue ground in the user's cable; repaired, still black) — through the Indivision AGA the `ddf-high` probe shows its picture, and it agrees with WinUAE on when sprites show; built: with a wide fetch sprites show from the earlier of DDFSTRT + 7 (+20 35 ns pixels) and the first visible plane data (`Denise::arm_sprites`, `data_from`, custom chunk version 17): agatest's sprite 0 now 4/20/36/64 pixels like the photo. The garbage, photographed with `tools/amiga_agaprobe.py planes` (eight planes, one 8-pixel stripe each, bands DDFSTRT $40, $3C, $40; four photos through the Indivision, 2026-11-13): the first $40 band is clean in the expected order; the $3C band shows a few lines of thin bars, then dense garbage in which parts of the stripes of planes 4–8 stay in place among white (overlapping planes) and black blocks, changing from frame to frame; in the second $40 band planes 1 and 3 are gone (black where red and blue were, the same in all photos) while planes 2 and 4–8 are back in place, and its last lines turn to garbage again. Desktop pacing, 2026-11-08: the emulation thread took the frame length from the picture's signal timing once at start; the Logophor's Panorama switches to the XGA timing (59.79 instead of 60.09 fields a second), so the radio ran 0.5 % ahead of the sound card (rate control at its +0.5 % limit, the buffer at 200 ms and growing). The length is now read every frame. The test `audio_rate` (`tests/tests/audio_rate.rs`) checks every machine's samples a frame against its signal timing: all within a few ppm; it found the Atari 2600 reporting its standard's lines instead of the field its program's VSYNC made (now the last field's lines; a cartridge-less console free-runs a third longer).
