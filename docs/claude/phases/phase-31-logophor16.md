Open: the Radio's own load (the 15 Hz waveform and the compose it causes, the scheduler), a Hermite resampler for 44.1 kHz stations, the desktop's pacing running 0.45 % faster than the sound card (the rate control at its limit, the buffer growing to AUDIO_MAX).
