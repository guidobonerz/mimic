## Backlog detail

- **Texas Instruments TI-99/4A** (added 2026-10-27 on the user's request, Phase 20): TMS9900 16-bit CPU at 3 MHz (new `cpu-tms9900`: workspace registers in RAM, context switching, CRU bit-serial I/O), TMS9918A/9929A video (reuse `chips-ti`; the video RAM is the only large RAM, the CPU sees 256 bytes of scratchpad plus the 16-bit console ROM), TMS9919/SN94624 sound (reuse the SN76489 of `chips-ti`), TMS9901 (interrupts, keyboard, joysticks, cassette), GROM (the Graphics ROMs with their auto-incrementing address and the GPL interpreter), cartridges (GROM + ROM, the 8 KB banking of later carts), 32 KB expansion RAM with its wait states, cassette, Peripheral Expansion Box with the TI disk controller (DSK images) and the Speech Synthesizer later; console ROM, GROMs and cartridge images user-supplied.

## Status

Begun 2026-11-10 on the user's request. User choices: the original ROMs from the user in `~/.mimic/roms/ti99/` (console ROM 8 KB, GROM 0–2 6 KB each); until they are there an own stub ROM, as with the ColecoVision. The 99/4A in NTSC (TMS9918A) and PAL (TMS9929A) by `--videoformat`; the 99/4 not. The phase gate first, then the Peripheral Expansion Box (disk controller, speech) as a second step of this phase. The TMS9900 is tested by own tests after the datasheet (no public test suite like the Harte tests), plus the boot with the real ROM.

Milestones:
Done 2026-11-10: M1 (`cpu-tms9900`, 19 tests), M2 (`chips-ti::tms9901`, `chips-ti::grom`), M3 (`machine-ti99` with the stub ROM, 8 tests) and most of M4/M5: with the real ROMs the title screen, the menu and TI BASIC come up and `PRINT 1+2` gives 3; Parsec runs from its C/G dumps (`media::ticart` bundles them in `open_cart`); 32 KB expansion; joysticks (`PortKind::Ti99Joy`); keymap; registered in the frontends (`tests/tests/ti99_boot.rs`, 2 tests). **The gate is met** (TI BASIC with real ROMs, own TMS9900 tests, a cartridge runs). The ROMs are in the user's `~/.mimic/roms/ti99` (with some cartridge dumps). Cassette (CS1) since the same day: WAV through `drives_other::apple_tape::AppleTape` with motor control (P6 high = motor on, P9 out, P11 in); TI BASIC `SAVE CS1` and `OLD CS1` round-trip with "DATA OK" (`ti99_boot.rs`). M6 part 1, the disk controller card, the same day: the FD1771 moved from `machine-trs80` to `drives_other::wd17xx` (shared; the TRS-80 re-exports it as `fdc`, its 7 ROM tests stay green), TI sector dumps in `media::dmk` (`Image::parse_ti_dsk`, `blank_ti_dsk`), `machine-ti99::disk` (DSR ROM at >4000, CRU >1100, inverted registers at >5FF0–>5FFE, the wait states until DRQ/INTRQ, the motor one-shot; slots DSK1–DSK3; `--option disk=on|off`, on when the ROM `ti99disk` is there); TI BASIC `SAVE DSK1.TEST` / `OLD DSK1.TEST` round-trip and the file is in the image's directory (`ti99_boot.rs`, 4 tests). M6 part 2, the Speech Synthesizer, the same day: `chips-ti::tms5220` (TMS5220 with the TMS6100 VSM: commands, status TS/BL/BE, the 16-byte FIFO for Speak External, the frame format and the coding table from the TI data manual; the voiced excitation and the interpolation are this project's model, as the manual gives no detail), at >9000/>9400 with `--option speech=on|off` (on when `SPCHROM.BIN`, role `ti99speech`, is there); Parsec talks when the game starts (`parsec_speaks`, the sound in `ti99-parsec-speech.wav` with `EMU_SNAPSHOT_DIR`). **M6 done.** Open: the real chirp excitation and interpolation shifts (by ear against hardware), the READY wait of speech accesses, double-sided disks only by image (the DSR's side handling untested), CS2 and the audio gate, GROM and VDP access timing beyond the 4 wait states, chips running ahead within an instruction (VDP status timing), the inverted (379) bank switching and the Mini Memory's RAM, Extended BASIC and Editor/Assembler not at hand, speech and disk (M6).
